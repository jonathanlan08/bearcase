# Response to the Astra review

An independent review of BearCase by another model ("Astra") was produced on 2026-09-06 against a snapshot of the tree taken on 2026-09-05, before the multi-provider chat commits (`9e8a7e3`, `bee5857`). The review, its screenshots, and the repairs it made in its own copy live in the untracked folder `bearcase.ai astra rewrite/audit/` (`REVIEW.md` and `changes-from-starting-snapshot.diff`). This page lists every finding and walkthrough item in the review and what happened to it.
